This lovely bunting is a great decoration for Easter.
Instructions:
Hoop 1 layer of cutaway stabilizer,
Stitch ‘V’ shaped outline object on stabilizer, then stop your machine,
Place bunting triangle over the V shape, and pin it to the stabilizer or use basting spray to keep it in place,
Stitch design.
